About the data

Bleachdle, Jujutsudle, Cloverdle, Hunterdle, Starwarsdle and Marveldle use the character databases from their sites. Jujutsudle and Hunterdle only include characters who have appeared in the anime so far (JJK through season 3, HxH through the Election arc). Harry Potterdle uses the site's character and spell lists, with details checked against the books. Flagdle's flags come from flagcdn.com, with each country's continent, location and area from the mledoze/countries dataset and recent population estimates. Planespottle uses Planespottle's photo list (Wikimedia Commons photos, credited to each photographer) plus extra aircraft with photos from Planespotters.net. Quote mode uses paraphrased lines. Series logos are public-domain text logos from Wikimedia Commons and remain trademarks of their owners.
