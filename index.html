YAHYADLE

Every dle in one cabinet. Random character each round, unlimited replays.

Bleachdle, Jujutsudle, Cloverdle, Hunterdle, Starwarsdle and Marveldle use the character databases from their sites. Jujutsudle and Hunterdle only include characters who have appeared in the anime so far (JJK through season 3, HxH through the Election arc). Harry Potterdle uses the site's character and spell lists, with details checked against the books. Planespottle uses a hand-compiled list. Quote mode uses paraphrased lines.
